FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

How does 3FX capture tab audio without lag?
3FX utilizes Google Chrome's native tabCapture API in combination with a high-performance Offscreen document and isolated AudioWorklet DSP nodes. Audio processing executes on dedicated hardware threads, bypassing the main browser render loop for zero audio glitching or perceptual latency.
Is my audio or browsing history private?
Yes, 100% private. 3FX operates entirely client-side. Zero audio streams, URLs, presets, or user data are ever uploaded to any server or third party. There are no tracking scripts, telemetry, or external network requests.
Can I customize settings differently for different websites?
Yes! With 3FX Site Memory, any adjustments you make to an effect rack (e.g. YouTube EQ, Spotify compressor, Netflix speech enhancer) are automatically saved to that domain and restored instantly when you reopen the tab.
What languages are supported?
3FX has 100% translation parity across 5 languages: English, Spanish (Español), French (Français), Korean (한국어), and Simplified Chinese (简体中文). You can switch your preferred language at any time in the Settings menu.
